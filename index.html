Short Bio

Xiangchun Chen is a Postdoctoral Researcher at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Jiannong Cao, IEEE Fellow. Previously, he received his B.E. degree in Computer Science and Technology from the Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology in 2021.

His research interests currently focus on reliability-aware AI agent execution and resource management across AI agents, LLMs, digital twins, and edge AI.

Research Focus

  • Reliability-aware AI Agent Execution. Dependable planning, execution, and adaptation for autonomous AI agents under dynamic conditions.
  • Resource Management & Edge AI. Efficient task offloading, scheduling, and resource coordination in collaborative edge environments.
  • LLM & Digital Twin Systems. Distributed LLM inference, state migration, and digital twin-assisted decision-making across edge infrastructure.
